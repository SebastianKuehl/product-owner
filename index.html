AI agents are great at writing code. They're terrible at deciding what to build next.

They start implementing without a plan. They don't track what's done. They merge half-finished work. They have no idea what version you're on. And when you ask "what's the status?" they make something up.

Product Owner fixes the layer above the code.

What Product Owner Does

Scope & Planning

Drop in a project idea or a feature request. Product Owner classifies it, creates structured docs/ with scope, milestones, features, and bugs — and keeps them current as the project evolves.

Work Decomposition

Every milestone, feature, and bug is broken into focused, agent-sized tasks with explicit acceptance criteria, linked IDs, and worker prompts ready to hand off. No ambiguous tickets.

Anvil Orchestration

Implementation work is delegated to Anvil agents — each in its own worktree, on its own branch, working from a precise prompt. Product Owner stays unblocked and ready for the next instruction.

Review & Merge Authority

Only Product Owner merges to main. It reviews Anvil completion handoffs against acceptance criteria, requests rework when needed, and never marks unfinished work as done.

Semver Releases

Merges that ship user-facing behavior get a version tag. Patch, minor, or major — decided by what changed. Release history lives in docs/releases.md. Tags only ever go on main.

README Governance

The README reflects what's merged and shipped — nothing more. Anvil agents may recommend README updates in their handoff, but only Product Owner makes the change. No docs drift.

How It Works

Clear chain of command.

Product Owner sits between you and the implementation agents. You give direction. Product Owner plans, delegates, reviews, and ships.

The Lifecycle

Every instruction runs through the same loop.

  1. Classify Is this a bootstrap, milestone, feature, bug, completion request, or status check? Product Owner decides before acting.
  2. Plan & Document Creates or updates docs/ — scope, milestones, features, bugs. IDs, statuses, priorities, dependencies. All tracked.
  3. Decompose & Prompt Breaks work into agent-sized tasks. Writes precise worker prompts in docs/prompts/ with acceptance criteria and forbidden actions.
  4. Orchestrate Launches Anvil agents in the background, each in a dedicated worktree. Records assignments in docs/progress.md. Stays available for your next instruction.
  5. Review When Anvil reports back, Product Owner verifies acceptance criteria, checks no forbidden files were touched, and either accepts or requests rework.
  6. Merge, Tag & Document Accepted work gets merged to main, tagged if it ships user-facing behavior, recorded in releases, and reflected in the README.
